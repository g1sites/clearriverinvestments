About Clear River

A different kind of investor — built for the long horizon.

Clear River Investments is a US-based, single family office focused on investments in growing businesses. With permanent capital, Clear River is focused on longer-term investment horizons than typical private equity funds.

We target small to mid-cap cash-flow positive companies in established sectors, with a mission to add operational expertise and increase cash returns. Our partners, operating companies, and employees share a common vision for sustainable long-term capital appreciation.

Principles

Three commitments that shape every partnership.

Patient Capital

Founded as a single family office, we do not have a traditional fund structure. That flexibility lets us own investments indefinitely and align with stable, cash-flowing companies built to last.

Partnership

A steady, strategic co-pilot. We help management teams manage and accelerate growth — seizing opportunities, mitigating risk, and preparing to scale.

Communication

Success is rooted in trust, integrity, and ethics. We prize open communication and transparency to build mutual understanding with every stakeholder.

Investment focus

Lower middle market. Established sectors. Real cash flow.

Operating in the lower middle market, Clear River's objective is to deliver measurable value to all stakeholders through enhancing and expanding profitable, sustainable business models.

In many cases our investment is the first time a management team has considered selling or seeking outside capital. We work alongside them to optimize operations, improve performance, and gain market dominance.
